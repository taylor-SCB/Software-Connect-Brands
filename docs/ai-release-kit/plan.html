Your four calls

Answered Nov 12, 2026. Everything else is mine to decide.

1 · Call List Where do the AI-drafted openers go? The Oct 2 build had its own Call List page. Since Nov 11, My day's Up next already is the ranked who-to-call list. Decided: the openers go on Up next, no second list.
2 · Receipts Can installers send receipts through the crew photo link? They don't have logins. A receipt from the link would be read by Claude but wait for an Owner or Admin before it counts as money spent. Decided: yes, held until an Owner or Admin approves.
3 · Site walk photos Keep the photos taken for a quote? Photos belong to jobs today, and a site walk happens before there is a job. Kept, they would sit on the quote and move into the job's photos when it's signed. Decided: keep them and move them into the job.
4 · Compliance Watch your subcontractors' insurance too, or only yours? Today only your own company's files live under Compliance. A sub's lapsed COI is the bigger risk on a job. Theirs would sit on their crew card. Decided: yours and your subs'.
