BDR Flow Redesign

A new BDR should open the app and know three things at once: who to call next, what happened last time, and the one next step. Today the app records what a rep did but not how it went. A voicemail and a real conversation both make someone "Contacted". This plan makes Connected a real step, shows every person as one lifecycle meter coloured by health, and strips each screen back to what a rep needs today. Nothing gets deleted. Nothing is built until you say yes.

The flow

Seven steps a first-week rep can say out loud

Attempted, Connected, Meeting Set, Meeting Completed, Quote Sent, Contract Sent, Won. The BDR owns the first four. The closer usually owns the last three. Both see the whole chain on the same meter, so a rep can watch how far their lead got after the hand-off.

You asked for this one

What "Connected" means

A two-way exchange with that actual person. Logging activity is an attempt. Connecting is the next step. Booking the meeting is the goal.

✓Counts as Connected

  • A call where you talked live, however short. That includes "not interested" and "call me next week".
  • They replied by text or email. The rep taps "They replied", because the app doesn't read inboxes yet.
  • A meeting that was held. Every past meeting already in the system counts.
  • Booking a meeting, or marking someone Interested or Not interested. You had to talk to do that.

–Is only an attempt

  • No answer, voicemail or busy
  • Front desk or "took a message"
  • Wrong number (the app also offers "Fix number")
  • A text or email you sent that got no reply
  • A no-show

Marketing sends stay what they are today: neither an attempt nor a connect.

Mock · after a call

How did it go with Kristen?

Tap an outcome. One tap saves it, with "Saved · Undo" for 10 seconds. Green-edged chips count as Connected.

The chips open on their own when a rep comes back from tapping Call or Text. The chip's words become the note, so a required note never blocks a log. Texts and emails get Sent · They replied · Meeting booked · Not interested. Meetings get Held · No-show · Rescheduled. Old history is never guessed: old calls count as attempts, old meetings as held, and Stats says "Talks counted from" the launch day.

How warm, in one word

The health score

Health is worked out each time a page loads, from data the app already keeps, and never stored, so it can be tuned without touching the database. It has one dial: the "quiet after" days in Settings → General (8 today), the same number that drives Gone quiet. Days are counted in your workspace's time zone.

New

Never touched. Grey outline, "Not tried yet". A fresh lead is never red.

Trying

Touched but never reached. Grey, turning amber when stalled. No number, never red: a score for someone nobody has spoken to would be made up.

Warm · 70+

Reached, recently touched, something booked.

Cooling · 40–69

Reached, but the touches or the next step are slipping.

Cold · under 40

A relationship that went quiet. In the demo, red lands on just 10 of 140 people, so red still means act now.

Won · Closed

Won shows ✓, then turns red if a payment is past due ("Owes $3,377 · 12 days late"). Closed is grey ×, with the reason.

Score = Recent touch (0–40) + Last talk (0–30) + Next step (0–30) − Drag (15)

PartHow it scores (quiet days Q = 8)
Recent touchAny call, text, email or meeting: 0–2 days 40 · up to 4 days 25 · up to 8 days 10 · longer 0. It hits 0 the day Gone quiet would fire.
Last talkTalked, replied or met: within 8 days 30 · within 16 days 20 · longer 10. Minus 10 after 5 or more tries since the last talk.
Next stepA meeting, call or follow-up booked ahead, or paperwork sent in the last 8 days, with nothing late 30 · something booked but something else late 15 · nothing booked 0.
DragMinus 15 when a quote or contract has been out more than 8 days with no touch since.

The reason sentence picks the first of these that applies: owes money · follow-up late · "Did the Nov 6 meeting happen?" · contract out and unsigned · quiet and nothing booked · tries since the last talk · talked and booked · still trying · not tried yet. A company takes its warmest person's health plus "2 of 7 reached". A Pipeline tile reads its listed people plus the paperwork drag. The colours are fixed on every white-label tenant and pass a colour-blind check in both looks. Colour is never the only signal: each state also has a mark and a word.

Only you can answer these

Your calls

Each has a recommendation. Reply with "approve the recommendations", or name the numbers you'd change.

  1. Approve this definition of Connected: a live talk, a reply by text or email, a held meeting, or booking one. Voicemail, no answer, front desk, wrong number, an unanswered text or email, and a no-show don't count.

    RecommendedYes, as written, with the one-tap chip words.

    Without it the app can't tell dialing from talking, which is the gap you named.

  2. Should Connected become a real step on the status ladder (Status filter, Stats funnel, rolling up to the company), or live only on the meter?

    RecommendedA real step, shipped in its own small release after the outcome chips have run a while.

    It adds one value and renames nothing. A database status value is hard to take back, so it goes last and small.

  3. Should "Contacted" read "Attempted"?

    RecommendedYes. Only the label; the stored value and CSV import stay the same.

    Next to Connected, "Contacted" after a voicemail tells a new rep something untrue.

  4. Should Meeting Completed be set automatically when a meeting is logged Held or ticked done?

    RecommendedYes, with a check when the meeting date is still ahead.

    7 of 13 Meeting Set tiles in the demo were meetings that had already happened and were never closed out.

  5. The follow-up box's "Meeting" tick sets Meeting Set today (your Oct 4 rule). Should it become "Try to book a meeting", a reminder that doesn't move the status, with real booking going through a Book meeting sheet?

    RecommendedYes.

    A rep who ticks it as a reminder fills Meeting Set and the Pipeline with meetings that don't exist.

  6. How should health show: a word plus a coloured dot or ring, or the 0–100 number everywhere?

    RecommendedWord and colour everywhere. The number only on hover or long-press, as a breakdown.

    Numbers on every row are the "coded" feeling you described.

  7. Should the health colours stay the same on every white-label tenant instead of following the partner's brand colour?

    RecommendedYes, fixed.

    Red has to mean "act now" for every customer, and these colours pass a colour-blind check that the app's current green / amber / red don't.

  8. Should people who've never been reached get a 0–100 score too?

    RecommendedNo. Show them as "Trying": grey, amber when stalled, never red, no number.

    One design tried it and painted 43 of 46 Contacted people red.

  9. What should a Member see first: the new "My day" or the Daily Rundown?

    RecommendedMy day, for Members only. Owners and Admins keep Daily Rundown with Team today on top.

    A BDR's first question is who to call now. Today the answer is about 2,250px down a phone screen.

  10. Old activities: leave them "not recorded" (counted as attempts), or run a one-time script that labels obvious voicemails from their notes?

    RecommendedLeave real data as it is. Run the script on the demo only. Neither ever guesses a connect.

    A wrong guess on a customer's history is worse than a blank.

  11. Team goals for tries, talks and meetings booked: who sets them?

    RecommendedOptional, per workspace, set by Owners and Admins in Settings → General. Until set, the rings show "vs last week".

    A scoreboard worth winning, without forcing a number on a team that doesn't want one.

  12. After how many tries with no connect should the app suggest parking a lead?

    Recommended6 tries. Parking only books "Try again in 30 days". No new status.

    Stops endless redialing without adding a step to the ladder.

  13. When a closer runs a meeting a BDR booked, who gets credit for the held meeting?

    RecommendedThe rep who booked it gets meetings held and show rate. The deal and its money stay with the deal's rep, as today.

    A BDR's job ends at a held meeting. This needs one new field recording who booked each meeting.

  14. Should Not interested and Lost ask for a one-tap reason?

    RecommendedYes, optional chips: Price · Timing · Has a vendor · No budget · Other.

    One tap, and it tells you why deals die.

  15. Can a Member bulk "Assign to me" a teammate's records?

    RecommendedYes, with a confirm that names the owner ("12 of these belong to Priya. Take them?") and an Undo.

    Today one mis-click silently takes a teammate's whole territory.

  16. The marketing email (the noreply@ sender) is separate from a one-to-one email. Should the Email button on a single contact's page become a plain one-to-one send that counts as a touch, while the marketing sender stays exactly as it is?Added by the review's critic

    RecommendedYes: a separate one-to-one Email (no template, no unsubscribe footer, not counted in the 40-a-day limit) that logs a Sent email. Marketing stays untouched.

    You said today that marketing sends must stay out of touches. This keeps that rule and still lets a rep's real email count.

  17. You ruled "everyone sees everyone". Should Members still open their lists, Pipeline and Calendar on "Mine", with Everyone one tap away?Added by the review's critic

    RecommendedYes: Mine is the default view, nothing is hidden, and Everyone is one tap away.

    Visibility doesn't change; only the starting view does. If you'd rather, Everyone stays the default and "Mine" becomes the first preset.

  18. Unowned leads: with no auto-assign, most new leads have no rep, so they'd never reach anyone's call list. Should Up next show "43 new leads nobody owns · Take 10"?Added by the review's critic

    RecommendedYes. Taking them is a manual claim with a confirm and Undo, so nothing is assigned automatically.

    It respects your no-auto-assign rule and still gets fresh leads called.

  19. "Build Quote" in the convert prompt puts the deal in Quote Sent before any quote exists. Should the stage move happen when the quote is actually sent instead?Added by the review's critic

    RecommendedYes: Build Quote leaves the deal at Meeting Completed until "Send quote". Manual Input still moves it straight away, as you designed.

    Otherwise the meter and the Pipeline show "Quote Sent" with no quote, which is the contradiction the review kept hitting.
