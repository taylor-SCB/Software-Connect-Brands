## headline
Taylor, here is one plan built from three proposals. It starts from the highest-scoring one ("BDR funnel first", 39 and 40 from the two judges) and takes the best ideas from the other two.

Today the app records what a rep did but not how it went. A voicemail and a real conversation both turn a lead "Contacted". In the demo, about 38 of the 46 people marked Contacted have never actually been reached.

The plan does three things:
1. Make "Connected" real with one-tap call outcomes: No answer, Left voicemail, Talked, Meeting booked.
2. Show every person as one lifecycle meter. Its length is how far along they are. Its colour is one plain health word: Warm, Cooling or Cold.
3. Rebuild each BDR screen to answer three questions in the same order: who is next, what happened last time, what is the one next step.

Nothing gets deleted. Anything a rep doesn't need today moves exactly one tap deeper.

The first release is about 4 days of bug fixes that rename and move nothing. Next comes the meter, built from data the app already stores, after you approve a mock.

## bdrFlow
THE SEVEN STEPS A FIRST-WEEK REP CAN SAY OUT LOUD
Attempted → Connected → Meeting Set → Meeting Completed → Quote Sent → Contract Sent → Won.
The BDR owns the first four. The closer usually owns the last three. Both see the whole chain on the same meter.

THE DAY, AS THE BDR LIVES IT

1. Open the app. A Member lands on "My day". Owners and Admins still land on Daily Rundown.
- One sentence at the top: "Morning, Marcus · 2 follow-ups late · 9 to call · Redbud has gone quiet 17 days."
- Three rings for the week: Tries 23 · Talks 6 · Meetings booked 2. They fill toward an owner-set goal. With no goal set, they show "vs last week".

2. "Up next": one ranked list with no group headings. Every row says in plain words why it is there, in this order:
- (a) "Met Nov 6 — how did it go?" The person is at Meeting Set, the date has passed, and nobody marked it Held or No-show. That is 7 of the 13 Meeting Set tiles in the demo today.
- (b) "Follow-up 4 days late".
- (c) "Due today".
- (d) "Talked 6 days ago — ask for the meeting". The person is Connected or Interested with nothing booked.
- (e) "New lead": mine, never touched.
- (f) "3 tries, never reached". Oldest try first. From 6 tries on, the row offers "Try again in 30 days".

Each row shows the avatar inside a health ring, the name, the company, the mini meter, the reason, and three buttons: Call · Text · Log. It shows 8 rows, then "Show all 14".

3. Tap Call and the phone dials the mobile first. When the rep comes back to the app, "How did it go?" is already open and set to Call. The choices are No answer · Left voicemail · Talked · Meeting booked · Not interested. "More" holds Busy · Front desk · Wrong number. One tap saves, and "Saved · Undo" shows for 10 seconds. A note is optional.

4. The next step is already picked, so it takes one more tap:
- No answer or voicemail: "Try again Thu" is ticked. The row drops off Up next until then. A dial with no answer is 3 taps in total.
- Talked: Book meeting (the main button) · Call back on… · "Interested" (one tap).
- Meeting booked: opens the Book meeting sheet (day chips, time chips, who else). It writes the calendar entry, sets Meeting Set and puts the person on the Pipeline: "Booked Tue Nov 17, 10:00 AM · Kristen is on your Pipeline →". About 6 taps from dial to booked.
- Not interested: an optional reason chip (Price · Timing · Has a vendor · No budget · Other) and "Check back in 90 days?".

The row folds into "Done today (n)". On a phone, a sticky bar reading "Next: Kelsey Dawson · Call" moves the rep down the list with one thumb.

5. Texts and emails work the same way: Sent · They replied · Meeting booked · Not interested. An email sent from a contact's own page counts as a Sent touch.

6. When the meeting day passes, the person goes to the top of Up next. Their Pipeline tile moves into "Happened — how did it go?" with three buttons: Held / No-show / Reschedule.
- Held moves them to Meeting Completed. It writes a Held meeting into their history, which counts as a connect and as a held meeting. That is the same moment the app already writes "Opportunity Created". Then it offers "Start a quote".
- No-show keeps Meeting Set and offers reschedule chips.

7. The hand-off. The closer moves Quote → Contract → Won by sending paperwork, as today. The BDR's meter on that person keeps filling, so the rep sees how far their lead got. Stats credits held meetings to the rep who booked them.

HOW THE EXISTING LADDER MAPS
One value is added. Nothing is renamed in the database.
- Not touched = NOT_ACTIONED. Unchanged.
- Attempted = CONTACTED. The stored value stays the same, and any logged touch still sets it. The label reads "Attempted" only if you agree.
- Connected = a new CONNECTED value between CONTACTED and INTERESTED. Talked, They replied or Held sets it. Until you say yes to it as a ladder step (Release 3), Connected appears only on the meter, the filters and Stats, worked out from the outcome data.
- Interested is a hand-set mark on the Connected step, not a segment of its own. Not Interested greys the track after Connected and adds an ×.
- Meeting Set is set automatically on booking, as today.
- Meeting Completed is set automatically on Held, if you agree. Today it is only set by hand.
- Quote Sent / Contract Sent / Won / Lost / Archived: unchanged. Paperwork drives them, as today.
- Marking someone Interested, Not interested or Meeting Set when they never reached Connected passes through Connected on the same day. You can't learn interest or book a meeting without talking. Old records are not back-filled.

## connected
WHAT IT MEANS
"Connected" means a two-way exchange with that actual person. Logging activity is an attempt. Connecting is the next step. Booking the meeting is the goal.

COUNTS AS CONNECTED:
- A call where you talked live with them, however short. That includes "not interested" and "call me next week".
- They replied by text or email. The rep logs it as "They replied", because the app doesn't read inboxes yet.
- A meeting that was held. Every past-dated meeting already in the system counts.
- Booking a meeting, or marking someone Interested or Not interested. You had to talk to do that.

DOES NOT COUNT (these are attempts):
- No answer
- Voicemail (left or full)
- Busy
- Front desk, gatekeeper or "took a message"
- Wrong number (this also suggests "Fix number")
- A text or email you sent that got no reply
- A no-show
- A call to a company's main line that names no person
Marketing sends stay what they are today: neither an attempt nor a connect.

HOW IT IS CAPTURED: ONE TAP, NOTHING TYPED
Outcome chips sit under the type chips, and they open on their own after a tel: or sms: tap.
- Call: No answer · Left voicemail · Talked · Meeting booked · Not interested. "More" holds Busy · Front desk · Wrong number.
- Text / Email: Sent · They replied · Meeting booked · Not interested.
- Meeting: Held · No-show · Rescheduled.
Tapping a chip saves right away. The chip's words become the note, so the required note never blocks a log. A 10-second Undo follows.

HOW IT IS STORED
A new field, Activity.outcome:
- NO_ANSWER: No answer, Busy
- VOICEMAIL
- GATEKEEPER: Front desk
- BAD_NUMBER
- TALKED: Talked. Also "Not interested" and "Meeting booked" on a call.
- REPLIED: They replied, and the text/email versions of Meeting booked and Not interested
- SENT
- HELD
- NO_SHOW
"Rescheduled" writes no activity. It moves the meeting entry.

A connect is an outcome of TALKED, REPLIED or HELD. A MEETING row with no outcome, dated today or earlier, also counts.

RULES
- The outcome applies to everyone ticked on that log. A group meeting that was held connects every attendee.
- On a company page, a "Front desk / main line" chip replaces the "I'm choosing not to link a contact" checkbox. It is always an attempt, and it still lands under the unlinked list to be claimed.
- A touch dated ahead gets no outcome until it happens.
- History is never rewritten or guessed. Old calls, texts and emails with no outcome count as attempts. Old meetings count as held. Stats says "Talks counted from <launch day>".
- Contact.lastConnectAt and Company.lastConnectAt are kept by the same database triggers that keep lastTouchAt, with the same "not in the future" rule. A company's value is its own or its people's, whichever is later.

WHAT IT DRIVES
- The meter's Connected segment
- The health score
- The "Talked — no meeting", "Not connected in 7 / 30 days" and "Never connected" filters
- Up next's "ask for the meeting" rows
- Tries vs talks on My day, Team today, Stats and the Activity report
- With your yes (Release 3), the CONNECTED step on the ladder

GONE QUIET IS UNCHANGED
Any touch still resets it, because a voicemail still means someone is chasing the lead. Health uses talks, so a lead nobody ever reaches still turns amber, and a relationship that stops talking still turns red.

## healthScore
WHAT HEALTH IS
Health is how warm the relationship is. It is kept separate from the stage: the meter's length is how far along someone is, and its colour is how warm they are.
- It comes from one function, leadHealth() in src/lib/health.ts.
- It is worked out when a page loads and never stored, so it can be tuned without a database change.
- It has one dial: the "quiet days" setting (Q) already in Settings → General, 8 today. That keeps Gone quiet and health in step.
- Days are calendar days in the workspace's time zone. This also fixes "Last touch: Today" showing for yesterday.

STEP 1: PICK THE STATE
The first four states get no score.
- NEW: never touched. Grey outline and "Not tried yet". A fresh lead is never red.
- TRYING: touched but never reached (no talk, reply or held meeting, and below Interested).
  - Normally grey, with the try count: "Tried 3× · last Tue · next try Thu".
  - It turns AMBER when the lead has stalled: a follow-up is late, or nothing is booked and the last try is more than Q days old. Example: "2 tries, last one 18 days ago — try again".
  - It never shows red and never shows a number, because a score for someone you've never spoken to is made up.
  - From 6 tries on: "6 tries, never reached — try a text or someone else at Ridgeline", plus "Try again in 30 days".
- WON: a green ✓ "Won". After signing, health means money. It shows red "Owes $3,377 · 12 days late" when a payment is past due, read from the same owed rows as the Owed to you tile. That ties the meter to STRATEGY job #1, chasing money.
- CLOSED: Not Interested, Lost or Archived. Grey ×, plus the reason if one was given.
- REACHED: talked, replied or met at least once, or at Interested / Meeting Set or later. These get a score.

STEP 2: THE SCORE (Reached only, 0–100)
Score = Recent touch (up to 40) + Last talk (up to 30) + Next step (up to 30) − Drag.

Recent touch: days since any call, text, email or meeting.
| Days since | Points |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | 40 |
| up to Q/2 (4) | 25 |
| up to Q (8) | 10 |
| more than Q | 0 |
It reaches 0 on exactly the day "Gone quiet" would fire.

Last talk: days since they last talked, replied or met.
| Days since | Points |
|---|---|
| within Q (8) | 30 |
| within 2Q (16) | 20 |
| longer ago | 10 |
Subtract 10 after 5+ tries since the last talk. The floor is 0.

Next step:
| What is booked | Points |
|---|---|
| A meeting, call or follow-up on the calendar for today or later, or a quote or contract sent within Q days, with nothing late | 30 |
| Something booked, but something else is late | 15 |
| Nothing booked, or only late items | 0 |

Drag: subtract 15 when a quote or contract has been out more than Q days with no touch since it went.

Bands:
- 70+ Warm
- 40–69 Cooling
- under 40 Cold

STEP 3: ONE REASON SENTENCE
The first one that applies wins:
1. "Owes $3,377 · 12 days late"
2. "Follow-up 4 days late"
3. "Did the Nov 6 meeting happen?"
4. "Contract out 18 days, not signed"
5. "Quiet 15 days · nothing booked"
6. "5 tries since you last talked"
7. "Talked 2 days ago · meeting Thu 10:00 AM"
8. "Tried 3× · next try Thu"
9. "Not tried yet"

THE LOOK
- On lists, tiles, Up next and People rows: an 8px dot in the health colour plus the reason sentence. No number.
- On the contact, company and opportunity headers: a 3px ring around the avatar plus the word chip ("Warm").
- The number appears only on hover, long-press or keyboard focus, as a breakdown: "Recent 25 · Talk 30 · Next step 0 = 55". This answers your "not so coded" ask.
- Colours are new fixed tokens, the same on every tenant and never the partner's brand colour:
| Token | Colour | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|---|
| --health-good | teal | #0d9488 | #0d9488 |
| --health-warn | amber | #d97706 | #d97706 |
| --health-risk | rose | #e11d48 | #be123c |
| --health-none | grey | #7e889c | #5b6473 |
- Unfilled track uses the same colour at 16–22%.
- The palette passes the colour-blind validator in both looks. The app's current --ok/--warn/--danger trio fails it, which is why these are new tokens.
- Meters sit on card surfaces, because Light amber on the grey page is under 3:1 contrast.
- Colour is never the only signal. Each state has a mark: ● Warm, ◐ Cooling, ▲ Cold, ○ Trying / New, × Closed, ✓ Won. The word or sentence always sits beside it. Words and numbers use text colours, never the health colour.
- Health words on screen: New, Trying, Warm, Cooling, Cold. Won and Closed are the stage's own words.

ROLL-UPS
- A company takes its warmest person's state, plus "2 of 7 reached".
- A Pipeline tile reads its listed people (not Do Not List), plus the paperwork drag.
- A rep shows Tries, Talks, Connect rate, Meetings booked, Meetings held and Show rate (held ÷ booked meetings that came due).

## lifecycleMeter
ONE COMPONENT USED EVERYWHERE
<LifecycleMeter> (src/components/lifecycle-meter.tsx), with <HealthRing> and <HealthDot>.
- It follows the existing Meter in ui.tsx: role="img" and a full aria-label, for example "Stage: Meeting Set, 3 of 7. Health: Cooling. Did the Nov 6 meeting happen?".
- It uses tokens only, so it reskins for every tenant and works in Dark and Light.
- In forced-colours mode, reached segments draw as solid fills and the rest as outlines.

SEGMENTS
Attempted · Connected · Meeting Set · Meeting Completed · Quote Sent · Contract Sent · Won.
- These are your ladder's own words, so there is no second vocabulary.
- Attempted reads "Contacted" unless you approve the new label.
- Interested is a small mark on the Connected segment ("Connected · Interested"). It is hand-set, not a step.

HOW IT FILLS
- Length = how far along. Every segment up to the current step fills.
- Colour = health. The fill takes the health colour: teal, amber or rose, or grey for never-reached prospects.
- Steps not yet reached are a faint track in the same colour.
- The next step gets a solid 1.5px outline and carries the one action.
- On the big header size, the current step breathes slowly. That stops under reduced motion.
- A newly reached segment fills with a 300ms sweep, for example the first time a rep logs "Talked".
- Not Interested, Lost and Archived: the reached segments turn grey, with an × after the last one and the reason.
- Won: all seven fill in the Won colour with a ✓. After signing, a thin second line appears: "Collected $0 of $6,754".

HEADER SIZE (contact, company, opportunity panel)
- Segments up to 640px wide and 10px tall, with a 3px gap.
- Each step's name sits under it in 12px. Reached steps show their date ("Oct 12", "Nov 2") from StatusChange. Steps the ladder passed through on the same day show no second date.
- Left side: the avatar in its 3px health ring and the word chip ("Cooling").
- Under it: the reason sentence, then a one-line caption, "7 tries · 2 talks · 1 meeting · last touch 2d". The caption replaces the six Activity overview tiles; their counts sit behind "Show counts".
- One next-step button that changes with the step:
  - Not touched or Attempted: Call
  - Connected or Interested: Book meeting
  - Meeting Set, before the date: "Meeting Tue 10:00 AM" with Reschedule
  - Meeting Set, after the date: Mark held
  - Meeting Completed: Start a quote
  - Quote Sent: Send quote / Customer said yes
  - Contract Sent: Copy signing link
  - Won with a deposit due: Send deposit invoice
- Tapping the next segment runs that action. Tapping a reached segment shows who moved it and when.
- Quote, Contract and Won are read-only on the meter ("Moves when the quote goes out"). That ends the "Contract Sent but No contracts yet" contradictions.
- The full 11-option status picker stays for corrections under ⋯ More → "Set status by hand", grouped Working it / Pipeline / Closed with small "auto" tags. It keeps the skipped-step date questions.

MINI SIZE (list rows, Up next rows, company People rows, Pipeline tiles)

- Beside it: the health dot.

ON A PHONE

## principles
- Every BDR screen answers three questions in the same order: who is next, what happened last time, and what is the one next step. Everything else stays reachable but quiet.
- One visual for stage and warmth. The lifecycle meter's length shows how far along someone is; its colour shows how warm they are. The rep learns it once and can read the lists, the record pages, the Pipeline and My day without being taught.
- Colour means "act now" and nothing else. Amber and red are reserved for health and late money. Stages use one brand ramp. A brand-new lead or a never-reached prospect is never red.
- Words over numbers. Health shows as a dot or ring with one plain word and one sentence. The 0–100 number appears only on record headers, on hover or long-press. Numbers, dates and phones use the normal font (Geist Sans with even-width digits), relative dates ("3d ago") and one 12-hour time format. Mono stays only for document numbers such as QUO-1000.
- Chip budget: at most one coloured chip per row, tile or card header. Zero counts, "N/A", "—" and any chip that repeats its column or the viewer's own name draw nothing. Measured targets: Contacts from 168 pills a page to under 20, Pipeline from 34 to under 10, the contact page from 15 cards to 5, the company page from 16 to 6.
- One action bar on every record. One filled main button follows the stage (Call → Book meeting → Mark held → Start a quote → Send contract → Send deposit invoice), then Call · Text · Email · ⋯ More. Delete only ever sits at the foot of More, behind its existing confirm.
- Filters are always the same shape: [Mine | Everyone] + search + 3 one-tap presets + "Filters (n)" + Sort. Every choice lives in the address bar and is remembered per person. "My people I haven't reached" goes from 5–11 clicks to 1.
- Nothing is removed. What a rep doesn't need today sits exactly one tap deeper (More, Columns, Filters, Show counts, Show numbers, row expand). A card appears only once it has something in it.
- Phone in a truck: 44px tap targets on touch screens, a sticky Call / Text / Log bar, sheets instead of dropdowns, nothing that scrolls sideways, and the after-call sheet opening on its own.
- Honest data: history is never guessed, talks are counted from launch day ("Talks counted from <date>"), the health score is never stored, and every AI or automatic step is still a suggestion a person accepts.
- Your rules hold. Nothing is renamed or moved without your yes. Every release goes live on its own after "Ready to go live. Push to production? Yes or No.". Every migration is safe for rows that already exist.

## decisionsForTaylor
- Q: Do you approve this definition of Connected? It counts a live talk, a reply by text or email, a meeting that was held, or booking a meeting. Voicemail, no answer, busy, front desk, wrong number, an unanswered text or email, and a no-show do NOT count.
  REC: Yes, approve it as written, along with the one-tap chip words.
  WHY: Without it the app can't tell dialing from talking. That is exactly the gap you named, and in the demo about 38 of the 46 "Contacted" people were never actually reached.
- Q: Should Connected become a real step on the status ladder (in the Status filter, the Stats funnel, and rolling up to the company), or live only on the meter?
  REC: A real step, but ship it as its own small release (Release 3) after the outcome chips have run for a while.
  WHY: It is the step you asked for, and it adds one value without renaming anything. Shipping it separately keeps the riskiest database change small and lets you see real connects first. A Postgres enum value is hard to take back.
- Q: Should "Contacted" read "Attempted"?
  REC: Yes. Only the label changes; the stored value and CSV import stay the same.
  WHY: Once Connected sits next to it, "Contacted" after a voicemail tells a new rep something untrue.
- Q: Should Meeting Completed be set automatically when a meeting is logged Held or ticked done? It would check first when the meeting date is still ahead.
  REC: Yes.
  WHY: 7 of the 13 Meeting Set tiles in the demo are meetings that already happened and were never closed out. Held meetings are the hand-off to quoting, and today Stats under-counts them.
- Q: The follow-up box's "Meeting" tick sets Meeting Set today (your Oct 4 rule). Should it become "Try to book a meeting", a reminder that doesn't move the status, with real booking going through the Book meeting sheet?
  REC: Yes.
  WHY: A rep who ticks Meeting as a reminder to try for one fills Meeting Set and the Pipeline with meetings that don't exist.
- Q: How should health show: the word plus a coloured dot or ring, or the 0–100 number everywhere?
  REC: The word and colour everywhere. The number only on record headers, on hover or long-press, as a breakdown.
  WHY: Numbers on every row are the "coded" feeling you described. A colour plus one plain sentence needs no explaining. The breakdown still lets you see why a score is what it is.
- Q: Should the health colours (teal / amber / rose) stay the same on every white-label tenant instead of following the partner's brand colour?
  REC: Yes, fixed.
  WHY: Red has to mean "act now" for every customer. These colours are also checked for colour-blind users in both looks, and the app's current green / amber / red trio fails that check.
- Q: Should people who have never been reached be scored on the same 0–100 scale?
  REC: No. Show them as "Trying": grey, turning amber when stalled, with no number and never red.
  WHY: A score for someone nobody has spoken to is made up. One proposal tried it and painted 43 of 46 Contacted people red. In the test run, red lands on just 10 people, all relationships that went cold.
- Q: What should a Member see first when they open the app: the new "My day" or the Daily Rundown?
  REC: My day, for Members only. Owners and Admins keep Daily Rundown with a new Team today strip on top.
  WHY: A BDR's first question is "who do I call now". Today the answer is about 2,250px down a phone screen, under owner money tiles.
- Q: Should old activities stay "not recorded" (counted as attempts), or should a one-time script label obvious voicemails from their notes?
  REC: Leave them as they are on real data, and run the script on the demo only. Neither option ever guesses a connect.
  WHY: A wrong guess on a customer's history is worse than a blank, and Stats says "Talks counted from <date>".
- Q: Should there be team goals for tries, talks and meetings booked, and who sets them?
  REC: Per workspace, set by Owners and Admins in Settings → General, and optional. Until set, the rings show "vs last week".
  WHY: The rings give a rep a scoreboard worth winning without forcing a number on a team that doesn't want one.
- Q: After how many tries with no connect should the app suggest parking a lead, and what does parking do?
  REC: 6 tries. Parking only books a "Try again in 30 days" follow-up. There is no new status.
  WHY: It stops endless redialing without adding a step to the ladder.
- Q: When a closer runs a meeting a BDR booked, who gets credit for the held meeting?
  REC: The rep who booked it gets the meetings held and the show rate. The deal and its money stay with the deal's rep, as today.
  WHY: The BDR's job ends at a held meeting, and their scoreboard should show it.
- Q: Should Not interested and Lost ask for a one-tap reason?
  REC: Yes, as optional chips: Price · Timing · Has a vendor · No budget · Other.
  WHY: It costs one tap, and it tells you why deals die. It is stored on the status change, so nothing else moves.
- Q: Can a Member "Assign to me" a teammate's records in bulk?
  REC: Yes, but with a confirm that names the owner ("12 of these belong to Priya. Take them?") and an Undo.
  WHY: It decides whose call list a lead lands on. Today one mis-click silently takes a teammate's whole territory.
- Q: Can Release 0 (bug fixes only) start now?
  REC: Yes. It renames and moves nothing. Each fix gets a browser-test step, and it goes live only after your usual yes / no.
  WHY: Dialogs opening off-screen, pages wider than the phone and menus running off the edge would undermine every later release, and you'd judge the meter through those bugs.
